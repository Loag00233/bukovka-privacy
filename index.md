# Bukovka Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22/11/2026

## Photos and your collection

Bukovka lets you build a personal collection of letters and inscriptions from photos. When you take a photo in the app or select one from your photo library, Bukovka uses Apple Vision to recognize text on your device.

When you save a sample, Bukovka stores a copy of the original photo, the cropped image, and any text, notes, and tags you add in the app’s local storage. Bukovka does not upload your photos or collection to the developer or to an online recognition service. The app does not require an account or use advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

## Permissions and deleting your data

Bukovka asks for camera permission when you choose to take a photo. You can revoke that permission in iOS Settings and continue using the system photo picker to select photos.

Your saved collection stays in the app until you delete the app from your device. To remove the local collection, choose Delete App in iOS. Photos in your system photo library are managed separately.

## TestFlight feedback and support

If you use Bukovka through TestFlight, Apple may share beta testing diagnostics and any feedback you choose to submit, including screenshots. If you email the developer, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this information to respond to you and investigate issues.
